Privacy Policy — Fifi Uploader

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

Fifi Uploader is a single-user personal tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Fifi's Paws and Play to upload their own original videos via the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

Through Google OAuth the app requests the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly. This allows uploading videos to the owner's own channel and reading back video metadata (title, privacy status, upload state) to verify uploads.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or share personal data from anyone other than the channel owner. No analytics, no advertising, no third-party sharing. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own machine.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends the app's access.
