Fifi Uploader

Fifi Uploader is a personal automation tool for the YouTube channel Fifi's Paws and Play (@fifispawsandplay).

It uploads original cat videos to the owner's own channel via the YouTube Data API, and reads back basic video metadata to verify each upload. It is used only by the channel owner. It does not collect, store, or share data from anyone else.
